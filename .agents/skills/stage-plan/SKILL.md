# Planning Stage

Use this stage to refine an approved discovery brief into small, valuable stories and a dependency-aware delivery plan. The Project Manager leads, with the Business Analyst clarifying requirements as needed.

Use `delivery-planning`, `project-traceability`, and `project-state`. Do not begin implementation, choose an architecture, or resolve missing product decisions by assumption.

Conclude with stories that meet the Definition of Ready, an incremental delivery order, material risks, and a recommendation to exit or move to Architecture.
