# Improve Stage

Use this stage after a release, completed increment, or material learning event. Project Manager leads, with Business Analyst, Tester, and DevOps contributing relevant evidence.

Use `project-traceability` and `project-state` to compare the intended outcome, acceptance evidence, defects, operational signals, and stakeholder feedback. Capture what to keep, improve, stop, defer, or add to the backlog.

Conclude with a short retrospective, prioritized follow-up items, owners, and a recommendation to exit or return to Brainstorm or Planning.
