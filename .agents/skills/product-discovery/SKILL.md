# Requirements Discovery

Use this skill for Business Analyst work when the goal, users, scope, or acceptance criteria are not yet clear. Do not use it to plan implementation tasks or make technical architecture decisions.

Start from the user's stated outcome. Identify the target users, problem, value, success measures, in-scope and out-of-scope behavior, constraints, dependencies, assumptions, non-functional requirements, and meaningful edge cases.

Write or update `projects/<project-name>/01-discovery.md` from `templates/01-discovery.md`. Express deliverable behavior as small, vertical user stories with stable IDs and observable acceptance criteria. Preserve unknowns as open questions; ask one focused question when an answer materially changes scope, behavior, or success criteria.

Before handoff, confirm every proposed story has business value, a priority, acceptance criteria, and enough context for delivery planning. Hand the approved brief to `project_manager`.
