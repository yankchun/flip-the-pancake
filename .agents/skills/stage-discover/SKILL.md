# Brainstorm Stage

Begin by asking the Product Owner for the starting point. Offer three clear paths: share an existing idea or brief, paste text or upload supporting material into the chat, or create a new idea from scratch. Review any supplied material before asking follow-up questions; do not make the Product Owner repeat information already provided.

Use this stage to explore a problem, target users, possible outcomes, assumptions, constraints, alternatives, and open questions. The Business Analyst leads. Involve the Project Manager for value and delivery implications, and the Solution Architect only when early feasibility or a technical constraint matters.

Use `product-discovery` and `project-state` to capture findings without treating unapproved ideas as commitments. Do not create a delivery plan, select technology, or implement work in this stage.

Conclude with a concise discovery brief, explicit open questions, and a recommendation to exit or move to Planning.
